# Sample demo questions

Use these to test retrieval and answers against the baseline travel/car policy wordings (when indexed locally or in the deployed demo).

## Document 1: Chubb “TravellerShield Plus – Policy Wording”

1. **Key terms:** Who can buy this policy regarding age and residency?
2. **Key terms:** What is the difference between Single Trip and Annual cover?
3. **Coverage:** What does it cover on a trip?
4. **Exclusions:** What are the main exclusions?
5. **Change policy:** If I need to change my dates or destination after buying, what do I do?
6. **Change policy:** Can I change my dates once I have started travelling?
7. **Renewal:** For Annual cover, how does renewal work and what happens if I do not renew on time?

## Document 2: esure “Travel Insurance Policy Wording”

1. **Key terms:** Am I eligible (age/residency), and are there limits such as maximum trip length?
2. **Coverage:** When does cover start—does cancellation cover start right away and when does travel cover begin/end?
3. **Exclusions:** What are the most common reasons a claim would be refused?
4. **Change policy:** If something changes (for example destination), how do I update the policy and who do I contact?
5. **Renewal:** For Annual cover, how do I renew and what happens if I let it lapse?

## Document 3: Zurich “Car Solutions Policy”

1. **Key terms:** What information must I keep updated to stay covered?
2. **Coverage:** What is the practical difference between Comprehensive vs Third Party/Fire & Theft vs Third Party Only?
3. **Exclusions:** What are the main exclusions owners should know?
4. **Change policy:** If I change address, how do I tell Zurich and when does the change take effect?
5. **Renewal:** What do I need to do at renewal, and do I get a cooling-off period after renewal?
